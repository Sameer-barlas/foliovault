How to Install the APK

Because FolioVault is distributed directly rather than through Google Play, Android asks you to confirm browser installation once.

Download the signed APK

Tap Download APK for Android above to fetch the latest release from GitHub Releases.

Allow installs from this source

Open the downloaded file. When Android prompts, tap Settings and enable Allow from this source for your browser or Files app.

Sign in with your authorized Gmail

Launch FolioVault and tap Continue with Google. Your phone binds automatically on first sign-in.

Play Protect notice: Direct APK installs may display a standard Google Play Protect scan prompt. Tap More details → Install anyway only when downloading from this official page. Need a device reset after upgrading or factory resetting your phone? Contact your administrator.
