Simple Setup

How to Get Started with Redoo

Begin reading your assigned editions in less than a minute with these simple steps.

Download Redoo

Tap the Download Redoo for Android button to get the verified APK file directly onto your smartphone.

Sign in with Google

Launch the Redoo app and tap Continue with Google using your authorized Gmail account. Your device pairs automatically.

Open & Read

Your personal library shelf unlocks immediately with all your assigned books ready for an effortless reading experience.

Installation Tip for Android: When opening the downloaded APK file for the first time, Android may prompt you with a standard confirmation (*"Allow installs from this source"*). Simply tap Settings and toggle allow. If a standard Google Play Protect notice appears, tap More details → Install anyway.
Reader Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about accessing and reading your books in Redoo.

How do books appear in my library?

Your educator, publisher, or administrator grants books directly to your authorized Gmail address. Once granted, they appear automatically in your Redoo library shelf.

What if I upgrade or change my phone?

Because Redoo securely binds to your physical device, simply contact your administrator or support to reset your device link whenever you switch to a new phone.

Can I read my books offline?

Yes! Once you open your assigned books during your active session, you can read smoothly without worrying about spotty internet connectivity.

Which Android versions are supported?

Redoo runs seamlessly on Android 7.0 (Nougat) and all newer versions, supporting smartphones and Android tablets alike.
